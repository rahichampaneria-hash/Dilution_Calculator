Plate Map Generator

Lay out a serial dilution series, or import a condition list from a spreadsheet, across a 96- or 384-well plate — with each well labeled directly, click-to-mark blank/control wells, freeform well/row/column labels, and CSV export.

Generate a plate map

Choose a plate format and direction, pick a start well, and enter your dilution series. The number of wells must fit the plate from your start well onward.

Well A1 is the top-left well. "Wells in series" counts the start well itself as the first (undiluted) well, so an 8-well series has 7 dilution steps after it.

Row/column headers and each individual well are editable — click a header to relabel a row or column (e.g. "1:10"), or click a well's "Note…" line to annotate it directly, for planning layouts beyond a single dilution series.

Generate a plate plan from a condition list

Upload a spreadsheet with a list of conditions/treatments — one row per replicate, or one row per condition plus a replicate-count column — and tell the tool which columns matter. Works with a messy real lab spreadsheet: pick the worksheet and columns that actually hold your data, everything else is ignored.

Tell the tool which column holds what — read the letters off the preview table above.

Leave "Replicate count column" blank if the sheet already has one row per replicate (e.g. the same condition listed twice for a duplicate) — that's how a plate-plan sheet is usually laid out already.

Conditions fill sequentially from the start well, wrapping into the next column (or row) once one fills — the same way you'd place a numbered list onto a plate by hand.

This tab doesn't yet support share links, since the plan comes from an uploaded file rather than typed-in numbers — export CSV to keep a copy.

Row/column headers and each individual well are editable, same as the Dilution Series tab — click a header to relabel it, or a well's "Note…" line to annotate it (pre-filled from your Note column, if you mapped one).
