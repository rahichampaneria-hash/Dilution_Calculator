Secondary Stock Solution Planner

For when a target concentration is so low that diluting straight from your primary stock would mean pipetting an unmeasurably small volume.

Plan a dilution

Enter your primary stock, your target concentration, and how much final solution you need. If a direct dilution would require pipetting less than your minimum comfortable volume, this tool designs an intermediate ("secondary") stock so every transfer stays practical.

If a single direct dilution keeps the transfer volume at or above your minimum, this tool just gives you that — no secondary stock needed. Otherwise it splits the dilution evenly across two stages.
